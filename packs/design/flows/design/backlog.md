summary: a design bead waits here until its question is clear enough to draft from

A design bead asks for a direction, not for code. Before it is sent to
`draft` its body says:

- the question, in one sentence;
- where to look: the files, commands, beads and issues it is about;
- what is already decided, and by whom, with their words where they matter;
- what is not wanted (what was ruled out, what to leave alone);
- the product: a design document, or a pass over the beads it names.
