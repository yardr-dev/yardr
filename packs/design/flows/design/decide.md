summary: a person reads the design and both notes, answers the open questions and takes the direction, or sends it back

For a person. What to read: the document on the bead's branch
(`docs/design/*<bead id>*.md`), the writer's note and the critic's note
(`yardr prime --bead <id>` prints the bead with every note in full). The open questions are what to answer; write the
answers in a note on the bead.

- to `approved` when the direction is taken: the document lands on the base,
  and the yardmaster shapes the beads it lists, with the answers;
- to `draft` with a note when it is not there yet;
- to `backlog` to park it.

A bead also comes here when the writer could not draft without an answer:
the note says what it needs.
